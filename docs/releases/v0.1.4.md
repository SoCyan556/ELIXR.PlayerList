# ELIXR Player List 0.1.4

- Current player list and desktop overlay now show you first, then actual VRChat friends, then other players. Friends and other players are alphabetical, with separate group headings. Recent retains newest-encounter-first ordering. Friendship metadata reuses existing native profile requests and is not inferred from community tags or saved in history.

- Recent Players now displays real saved encounters rather than an empty placeholder. Records survive instance changes and app restarts, are deduplicated by VRChat ID, and show newest encounters first.
- History is local and separated by signed-in account, capped at 1,000 players and 30 days. Search results and incomplete IDs are not recorded. Tags and portraits are snapshots from the last captured encounter, not a fresh safety assessment.
- Captures join/leave events even when a player leaves before the initial roster snapshot. History is saved before the main window closes and is not sent to the desktop/VR overlay.
- Stored FewTags labels on active records now appear even when TextActive/BigTextActive is false. Retired records remain excluded; false flags do not invent labels. The old six-label truncation is removed, with a 64-label safety limit.
